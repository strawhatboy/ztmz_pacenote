v2.99.99.41-3.0-rc13

* Corrects the BeamNG engine-damage projection: turbo/supercharger induction damage and generic impact damage remain in raw diagnostics but no longer falsely light the engine component or trigger an engine-failure call.
* Adds live BeamNG.drive Rally Mode co-driver audio. Pacenote Tool now follows BeamNG's current pacenote timing and translates the game's native pacenote data at runtime; saved stage exports are for diagnostics and coverage checks.
* Adds BeamNG Rally countdown calls and bridge version checks. Rally bridge 1.1.10 repairs outdated installs, activates dashboards from the first countdown packet, supplies an initial vehicle-damage baseline so the Car Damage dashboard is visible for an undamaged car, and carries dashboard data that OutGauge does not expose: correctly oriented, normalized 60 Hz steering input, max-gear count, analog handbrake, and G-force. Diagnostics report both OutGauge and Rally bridge UDP listeners.
* Refines the Car Damage dashboard with a configurable short alert for race-start and changed components, while keeping normal component status quieter during driving without hiding persistent warning and critical states. BeamNG middle-left and middle-right body damage now maps to the corresponding door.
* Adds opt-in BeamNG vehicle-damage calls. Bodywork changes are combined into one collision call, while radiator, water-pump, and engine faults use dedicated system tokens with repeat protection. Voice packages can add matching recordings; missing clips use the existing caution cue.

v2.99.99.40-3.0-rc12

* Consolidates improvements since v2.99.99.36-rc9: the .NET 10 and WPF-UI update, safer game telemetry ports, and more reliable UDP and audio handling.

* Improves update delivery when the application starts minimized: an available update is announced through the tray icon and remains available from the tray menu.
