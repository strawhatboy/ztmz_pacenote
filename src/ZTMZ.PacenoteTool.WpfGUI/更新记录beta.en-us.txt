v2.99.99.56-3.0-rc28

* Fixes dashboard labels following the selected WPF/Avalonia language in browser, in-game and standalone rendering, including live language changes.
* Fixes clean WPF release builds omitting the WRC 7/9/Generations provider; verifies bundled dashboard scripts, translations and fonts against source.
* Adds DiRT Rally 2.0 turbo boost pressure for the verified Windows build, using the native HUD reading and preserving vacuum and zero. The gauge hides for non-turbo cars or unavailable data.
* Adds a compact Liquid Glass dashboard with speed, gear, shift lights, pedals, steering and handbrake. Hybrid, boost/intake pressure, engine temperatures, time delta and key damage indicators appear only when data is available, with unused space collapsed.
* Adds in-game background refraction and diffusion on DX9, DX10, DX11, DX12 and Vulkan, with per-dashboard effect, tint opacity, refraction and diffusion controls. Defaults of 8 refraction and 4 diffusion soften the backdrop and reduce edge distortion. Separate overlays and browser dashboards use a translucent fallback; other dashboards are unaffected.
* Adds shared engine temperature and boost/intake pressure readings for RBR and BeamNG.drive, retaining pressure-reference information and hiding invalid or stale readings instead of presenting missing data as healthy or zero.
* Fixes modern WRC 2025 dashboard rendering when the maximum RPM is unavailable. A failing in-game dashboard script no longer prevents other dashboards from rendering, and retries automatically.
* Fixes missing, unreadable or invalid RBR pacenote definition files preventing startup. Keeps ZTMZ running, clears stale mappings and shows a warning when a stage needs the unavailable definition; restoring or reselecting a valid file restores mapping support.
* Fixes the installer ignoring the installation directory saved in the application registry; keeps the uninstaller destination synchronized and preserves explicit /DIR overrides and separate ACR experimental installations.
* Adds hybrid indicators styled individually for the four modern WRC dashboards: integrated battery gauges, lightning icons and circular boost/recharge progress, with state/map readings that hide automatically when telemetry is unavailable.
* Adds WRC Generations Rally1 hybrid telemetry: battery charge/energy, boost/recharge progress, boost readiness and activation, regeneration phase and selected map, available to desktop and browser dashboards.
* Adds WRC Generations body and mechanical damage data, including hybrid-system condition, plus collision and engine damage calls with pause/restart/reconnection safeguards.
* Adds WRC 7 mechanical damage and wear data, with collision and engine warnings that suppress old damage after restart, pause or reconnection.
* Adds experimental WRC 9 support for the verified Windows Steam build in WPF and Avalonia, including live pacenotes, countdown calls, basic telemetry and in-game dashboards, with normal finish, penalty and retirement detection.
* WRC 9 voice playback requires enabling and connecting the voice bridge after each game launch; enabling the dashboard alone does not connect it. Original game speech remains enabled; some pacenotes, tyre punctures and manual car-reset events are not yet supported.
* Expands WRC 9 telemetry with steering, clutch, gear/RPM limits, shift warning, stage progress, four wheel speeds, brake temperatures, suspension and G forces. Adds body/mechanical damage displays and collision/engine damage calls, without repeating existing damage after pause, restart or reconnection.
* Fixes shift-light progression in WRC 7, WRC 9 and WRC Generations: green/yellow/red cover the final 1000 RPM before the native warning point, with flashing red at that point instead of skipping solid red.
* Adds settings search in the title bar to quickly find and open relevant settings.
* Checks installed co-driver voice packages for updates at startup and shows update badges on the co-driver navigation entry and affected packages.
* Adds update counts and badges for game Mods, with guidance for updating older Mods managed by JSGME.
* Fixes finish congratulations playing after terminal damage, retirement or a timeout in some games, and adds retirement calls for explicit retirements in DiRT Rally 2.0, BeamNG, WRC 7, WRC Generations and RBR (RSF Practice, Hotlap and online racing); restarting a stage or an unknown result no longer triggers congratulations.
* Improves RBR pacenote detection by prioritizing the Luppis pacenotes actually loaded by the game, reducing false warnings and misleading reinstall advice.
* Adds experimental MoTeC telemetry export: saved race telemetry can be exported as .ld / .ldx files for analysis in MoTeC i2.
* Adds preset accent colors and lets you click the version number on the About page to copy it.
